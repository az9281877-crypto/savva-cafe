# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind CSS. GSAP is reserved for the approved motion stage. The project uses npm. The published experience is intended to remain static and requires no runtime backend.

## Users

Arabic-speaking residents and visitors in Madinah checking SAVVA on a phone, with English available as a deliberate alternate language.

## Product Purpose

Turn SAVVA's Instagram-like atmosphere into a calm vertical visit: discover the café, browse truthful menu information, understand the space, then open Google Maps, Instagram or WhatsApp.

## Positioning

“A DAY IN SAVVA” follows the sensory shift from Madinah heat and a cold glass to an evening terrace. The site should feel like the café's digital continuation rather than a generic café template.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Arabic and RTL are the default; English and LTR are available at `/en/`.
- Business information lives in `src/content/site.ts`.
- Unknown prices remain unknown. No reviews, ratings, awards, founder story or menu items may be invented.
- Gallery media is local. There is no Instagram API, account system, saved likes, form storage, database or backend.
- Night atmosphere uses `Asia/Riyadh`; the approved working window is 18:00–07:00.
- Accessibility includes semantic HTML, keyboard focus, sufficient contrast and a complete reduced-motion experience.

## Brand Commitments

Savva | ساڤا, the supplied palette and copy, a cinematic and tactile pace, and the contrast between daytime heat and a cool evening. Avoid generic beige café templates, purple gradients, excessive glass, repetitive cards and stock imagery unrelated to SAVVA.

## Evidence on Hand

The brief supplies the business name, contact details, links, operating hours, menu names, selected prices, six Arabic story lines and the intended gallery subjects. No logo files or café photographs are present yet. The Arabic address and newly translated interface copy require owner review before publication.

## Product Principles

- Make the Arabic experience primary in structure, content and reading order.
- Keep every commercial fact traceable to the supplied brief.
- Spend visual complexity on one continuous day-to-night story.
- Preserve natural scrolling and fast access to menu, maps and WhatsApp.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must work with keyboard navigation, reduced motion, touch input and mixed Arabic/Latin content across 375, 390, 430, 768 and 1440 pixel viewports.
